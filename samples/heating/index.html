What we do

Fix it, replace it, or keep it from breaking

Six jobs make up almost everything we do. Prices below are the full price, not a starting price.

  • Furnace repair

    No heat, strange noises, a pilot that will not stay lit. We find the fault, quote the repair, and fix it on the same visit when the part is on the truck.

    $89 visit, credited to the repair

  • Air conditioning repair

    Weak airflow, ice on the lines, water pooling by the indoor unit, a breaker that keeps tripping. Same flat diagnostic, same credit.

    $89 visit, credited to the repair

  • Furnace and AC replacement

    When a repair stops making sense, we show you two or three options at different prices, in writing. Most installs finish in one day.

    Free in-home estimate

  • Tune-ups

    A 21-point check, a new filter, and a clean of the burners or coils. Catches the small thing in October that would have been a no-heat call in January.

    Heating or cooling $129, both $219

  • Heat pumps and mini-splits

    For rooms the ducts never reached, and for homes moving off oil or propane. We size the system before we quote it, not after.

    Free in-home estimate

  • Maintenance plan

    Two tune-ups a year, priority booking, and 15% off repairs. Plan members get a call back within the hour on a no-heat night.

    $16 a month, cancel any time

Why Maple Ridge

  1. Fact 1

    You see the full price before any work starts, on paper or on your phone. If we find more once the panel is off, we stop and ask. No surprise on the invoice.

  2. Fact 2

    Answer by tomorrow

    Every quote request gets a real reply by the end of the next business day. A person writes it, with a day and a time window you can plan around.
